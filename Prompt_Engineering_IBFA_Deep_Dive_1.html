Introduction to Language Models

Before you can master prompts, you need to understand what's happening "under the hood" — even at a beginner level.

What Is a Language Model?

Think of a language model as an extremely well-read assistant who has read billions of documents — textbooks, research papers, financial reports, news articles, and code. When you give it a starting sentence (a "prompt"), it predicts the most helpful continuation based on everything it has learned.

Simple Analogy

Imagine playing a word-guessing game: you say "The stock market ___" and everyone guesses the next word. A language model is like a player who has read millions of finance books and can very accurately predict: "...fell 3% today due to rising interest rates."

How Does a Prompt Work?

Prompt = Instructions + Context + Examples + Output Format
🎓 Finance Example — Same goal, different prompts
❌ Vague Prompt

Tell me about DCF.

AI Response (Generic)

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) is a valuation method used to estimate the value of an investment based on expected future cash flows...

↓
✅ Precise Prompt

You are a senior investment banking analyst at Goldman Sachs. Explain DCF valuation to a 1st-year analyst using a simple 3-year example with a tech startup generating $1M, $1.5M, $2M in free cash flows. Use a 10% discount rate. Show the calculation step by step.

AI Response (Specific & Actionable)

Year 1: PV = $1M / (1.10)¹ = $909,090 | Year 2: PV = $1.5M / (1.10)² = $1,239,669 | Year 3: PV = $2M / (1.10)³ = $1,502,629 | Total DCF Value = $3,651,388...

⚠️ Critical Warning for Finance Students

AI can hallucinate financial data — it may invent stock prices, revenue figures, or legal clauses that sound real but are wrong. Always verify numbers from Bloomberg, FactSet, or official filings. Never submit AI-generated financials to a client without fact-checking.

Best Practices to Write Prompts

Eight proven techniques that will immediately improve every prompt you write.

The CRISP Framework

C — Context  |  R — Role  |  I — Instructions  |  S — Style  |  P — Purpose
🏦 Applying CRISP — Cover Letter Example
Basic Prompt (no CRISP)

Write a cover letter for an equity research job.

CRISP Prompt — Much Better

[ROLE] You are a professional career coach specializing in investment banking.
[CONTEXT] The applicant is a 5-year equity research analyst at a mid-size firm with CFA Level 2, specializing in TMT sector.
[INSTRUCTIONS] Write a compelling cover letter for a Senior Equity Research Analyst position at Morgan Stanley.
[STYLE] Professional, confident, concise — 3 paragraphs, under 300 words.
[PURPOSE] The goal is to highlight TMT expertise and get an interview callback.

The 8 Rules of Effective Prompting

  1. Be Specific About the Outcome
    ❌ Vague

    "Explain financial modelling"

    ✅ Specific

    "Explain a 3-statement financial model to a first-year IB analyst. Include what each statement is and how they connect. Use bullet points."

  2. Assign a Role to the AI
    ❌ No Role

    "Review this pitch deck"

    ✅ With Role

    "You are a Senior VC Partner at Sequoia Capital with 15 years of experience. Review this pitch deck from the investor's perspective. Focus on market size, team strength, and unit economics."

  3. Specify Format & Length

    Add: "Respond in a table", "Use bullet points", "Max 200 words", "Include examples for each point"

  4. Use Positive Language
    ❌ Negative

    "Don't make it too long and don't use jargon"

    ✅ Positive

    "Keep it under 150 words. Use plain language that a non-finance person can understand."

  5. Provide Examples (Few-Shot Prompting)

    Give the AI 1-2 examples of what good output looks like. "Write a deal summary like this example: [your example]. Now do it for: [new deal]"

  6. Use Delimiters to Structure Your Prompt
    Prompt StructureUse """ or ### to separate sections: Analyze the following company: """ Company: TechStart Inc. Revenue: $5M (2023) Industry: SaaS B2B Stage: Series A """ Provide a 5-bullet investment thesis from a VC perspective.
  7. Ask for Step-by-Step Reasoning (Chain of Thought)

    Add "Think step by step", "Walk me through your reasoning", or "Show your work" — especially for financial calculations.

  8. Iterate & Refine

    Prompting is a conversation. If the first response isn't right, say "Make it more formal", "Add more numbers", or "Shorten the second paragraph". You don't need to start over.

Part B: VBA Macros with AI

VBA Macros automate repetitive tasks. Normally you need to know programming — but with ChatGPT, you just describe what you want in plain English.

How to Use ChatGPT for Macros (Step by Step)
  1. Open ChatGPT and describe your task clearly
  2. Copy the VBA code it gives you

  4. Click Insert → Module, paste the code

Macro Examples for Finance

Scenario 1: Auto-format a financial report

Prompt to ChatGPT

Write a VBA macro for Excel that:
1. Makes all header cells in Row 1 bold, dark blue background with white text
2. Auto-fits all column widths
3. Adds alternating row colors (light blue and white) for rows 2 to 100
4. Adds a border around the entire data range (A1 to M100)

VBASub FormatReport() ' Format headers With Rows(1) .Font.Bold = True .Interior.Color = RGB(0, 70, 127) ' Dark blue .Font.Color = RGB(255, 255, 255) ' White text End With ' Auto-fit columns Cells.EntireColumn.AutoFit ' Alternating row colors Dim i As Integer For i = 2 To 100 If i Mod 2 = 0 Then Rows(i).Interior.Color = RGB(235, 244, 255) Else Rows(i).Interior.Color = RGB(255, 255, 255) End If Next i End Sub

Prompt

Prompt

Advanced Prompting Techniques

Once you've mastered the basics, these techniques will make you a true power user.

1. Chain of Thought (CoT) Prompting

Ask the AI to show its reasoning step by step. Critical for financial calculations where you need to verify each step.

🧮 Finance Example: DCF Calculation with CoT
Chain of Thought Prompt

Walk me through a DCF valuation step by step. Think carefully at each stage.

Company: TechVentures Ltd
- Free Cash Flow Year 1: $2M
- FCF Year 2: $3.5M
- FCF Year 3: $5M
- Terminal Growth Rate: 3%
- WACC: 12%

Step 1: Discount each year's FCF
Step 2: Calculate Terminal Value
Step 3: Discount Terminal Value
Step 4: Sum to get Enterprise Value

Show all calculations and explain the formula used at each step.

2. Role + Audience Stacking

Assign both a role to the AI AND specify the target audience. This shapes tone and depth perfectly.

AI Role → defines expertise level | Target Audience → defines communication style
Examples
AI RoleAudienceResult
Senior IB Analyst"a first-year analyst"Educational, explains jargon
VC Partner"a non-technical founder"Business-focused, avoids finance jargon
Credit Risk Manager"the CFO"Strategic, data-heavy, executive summary style
Equity Research Analyst"retail investors"Plain English, analogies, no jargon

3. Persona + Constraint Prompting

📝 Assignment: Cover Letter with Full Constraints
Full Constraint Prompt

PERSONA: You are an expert career coach specializing in investment banking recruitment, with knowledge of what MDs at Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan look for in candidates.

TASK: Write a cover letter for this candidate:
- 5 years equity research experience, TMT sector
- CFA Level 2 passed
- Top performer in current role (20% above team average)
- Targeting: Senior Equity Research Analyst at a bulge bracket bank

CONSTRAINTS:
- Exactly 3 paragraphs
- Maximum 280 words
- Formal, confident tone (not arrogant)
- No clichés like "I am writing to express my interest"
- Open with a compelling hook that references a specific market insight

OUTPUT FORMAT: Write the full letter, then provide a 3-bullet critique of what makes it strong.

4. Iterative Refinement Workflow

  1. First Pass: Get a draft — don't overthink your first prompt
  2. Evaluate: Read the output — what's missing? What's off?
  3. Refine: "Make the second paragraph more quantitative", "Add a risk section", "Shorten by 30%"
  4. Fine-tune: "Change the tone from formal to conversational", "Add 2 specific data points"
  5. Final check: "Review this response for accuracy and any claims that need verification"

5. Structured Output Prompting

Tell the AI exactly what format you need the output in:

Structured Output Prompt

Analyze the following startup for investment potential.

Company: [Description]

Provide your analysis in EXACTLY this format:

VERDICT: [INVEST / PASS / MORE DILIGENCE]

STRENGTHS:
• [Point 1]
• [Point 2]
• [Point 3]

• [Point 1]
• [Point 2]

KEY QUESTIONS FOR FOUNDERS:
1. [Question]
2. [Question]
3. [Question]

COMPARABLE COMPANIES: [Company 1], [Company 2]

6. The "Red Team" Technique

Advanced IB Use Case

After getting an investment thesis or memo, ask the AI to attack it:

Red Team Prompt

Here is an investment thesis I wrote for XYZ Company: [paste thesis]

Now act as a skeptical Senior Partner who is trying to find every flaw in this thesis. Generate 5 strong counterarguments that could convince an investment committee to REJECT this deal. Be specific and use financial reasoning.
