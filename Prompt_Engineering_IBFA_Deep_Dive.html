From writing better emails to analysing stocks — AI tools are transforming Investment Banking and Finance. This session gives you the theory AND the exact prompts to use right now.

What Is a Prompt?

A prompt is your starting instruction to an AI model. Think of it as giving directions on a map — the more precise and detailed, the closer the AI gets to exactly what you need.

"Acting as a [Role], perform this [Task] in this [Format]" The Master Formula — works for 90% of professional use cases

🎭 Role

Who is the AI pretending to be? "Equity research analyst", "CFO", "investment banker"

📋 Task

What should it do? "Write a cover letter", "Analyse this company", "Create a macro"

📐 Format

How should the output look? "In bullet points", "As a table", "In 300 words"

Best Practices & Prompt Refinement

Great prompts don't come out perfectly the first time — they're refined through iteration. Here are the 5 core refinement techniques with live demos.

The 5 Refinement Techniques

Technique 1 — Relevance: Remove what you don't needDemo 1
▶ First Prompt (Too Broad)
Tell me about Google Inc.
▶ Refined Prompt (Specific + Relevant)
Acting as a financial analyst, give me a brief overview of Alphabet Inc. (Google's parent company) covering only: founding history, corporate structure, revenue model, and market dominance. Keep it under 200 words in bullet format.
✦ Why It Works
The first prompt returns a general Wikipedia-style paragraph. The refined prompt sets a role (financial analyst), defines exactly what topics to include, sets a word limit, and specifies bullet format. The AI now knows exactly what to write — and what to leave out.
💡 Classroom Tip
Show students the two outputs side by side. The first is a long paragraph with general history. The second is a tight, analyst-ready brief. Ask: "Which would you actually use in a client presentation?"
Technique 2 — Set Context: Tell the AI who YOU areDemo 2
▶ Without Context
What are the key risks of investing in Tesla?
▶ With Context
I am a buy-side equity analyst at a hedge fund evaluating a potential long position in Tesla (TSLA). List the top 5 investment risks I should present to my investment committee, in order of severity. For each risk, include: the risk name, a one-line description, and the potential impact on the stock price. Format as a numbered list.
✦ Why It Works
Context transforms the AI's output from generic consumer advice into professional, committee-ready analysis. The role ("buy-side analyst at a hedge fund"), the decision context ("long position"), and the output structure (numbered list with 3 sub-items) all work together to produce boardroom-quality content.
💡 Classroom Tip
Run both prompts live and compare. Point out that "I am a financial analyst" is the single biggest upgrade most students can make to any prompt — it shifts the tone and depth of the entire response.

Chain-of-Thought Prompting

For complex financial analysis, break the task into sequential steps. Each response builds on the previous one — like a conversation.

Chain-of-Thought — Multi-Step Company AnalysisDemo 3
▶ Step 1: Set the Scene
I am a financial analyst. I need to build a brief analysis report on Apple Inc. (AAPL). Step 1: Give me a company overview — 5 bullet points covering business model, revenue segments, and market position. Keep it concise.
▶ Step 2: Add Financial Data (after reviewing Step 1)
Good. Now add a financial summary section covering: revenue growth (last 3 years), profit margins, key financial ratios (P/E, EV/EBITDA), and one risk factor. Keep the same bullet format.
▶ Step 3: Create the Final Summary
Now combine everything above into a one-page analyst note. Use headers: Overview, Financial Snapshot, Key Risk, Investment Thesis. Keep the total length to 300 words maximum.
💡 Classroom Tip
Show that this 3-prompt sequence produces a professional analyst note in under 2 minutes. A junior analyst might spend 2 hours doing the same research. This is the power of iterative prompting in finance.

ChatGPT + Excel: Formulas & VBA Macros

Part A: Excel Formula Generator  No Plugin Needed

Paste the formula prompt into ChatGPT and copy the result directly into Excel. Works on the free tier.

Prompt 1 — Build a Dynamic Financial FormulaDemo 1
▶ Prompt
I am working in Excel. I have a financial model with this structure: • Column A: Company names (A2:A20) • Column B: Revenue (B2:B20) • Column C: Net Profit (C2:C20) • Column D: Number of shares outstanding (D2:D20) Write me an Excel formula to: 1. Calculate the Profit Margin % for each company (Net Profit / Revenue × 100) 2. Calculate Earnings Per Share (EPS = Net Profit / Shares) 3. Use IFERROR to handle any division-by-zero errors gracefully Provide the formula for row 2 that I can drag down. Explain each formula in one sentence.
✦ What ChatGPT Produces
Profit Margin (put in E2, drag down):
=IFERROR((C2/B2)*100, "N/A")
Divides Net Profit by Revenue and multiplies by 100 to get percentage; returns "N/A" if Revenue is zero.

EPS (put in F2, drag down):
=IFERROR(C2/D2, "N/A")
Divides Net Profit by shares outstanding; returns "N/A" if shares data is missing.

Prompt 2 — Advanced Lookup Formula for Financial ModelsDemo 2
▶ Prompt
I have an Excel file with two sheets: • Sheet1: "Companies" — Column A has company names, Column B has their sector • Sheet2: "Financials" — Column A has company names, Column B has revenue I need a formula in Sheet2 Column C that looks up each company's sector from Sheet1. Use INDEX-MATCH (not VLOOKUP — VLOOKUP can break if columns are inserted). Show the formula for C2 and explain how INDEX-MATCH works in one paragraph for a beginner.
✦ Expected Output
Formula for Sheet2 C2:
=IFERROR(INDEX(Sheet1!B:B, MATCH(A2, Sheet1!A:A, 0)), "Not Found")

Explanation: MATCH finds the row number where A2 (your company name) appears in Sheet1 column A. INDEX then retrieves the value from Sheet1 column B at that row number. This is more robust than VLOOKUP because the lookup column doesn't need to be first, and adding columns to your sheet won't break it.
💡 Classroom Tip
This is a genuinely useful IB formula — financial models frequently join data from multiple sheets. The AI also explains WHY INDEX-MATCH is preferred over VLOOKUP, which is exactly what an experienced analyst would teach. Ask: "Could you have written that explanation yourself?"

Part B: ChatGPT + VBA Macros  Automate Everything

VBA (Visual Basic for Applications) is the automation language built into Excel. Instead of learning to code it yourself, describe what you want in plain English and ChatGPT writes the macro.

How to Run a VBA Macro in Excel
  1. Press Alt + F11 to open the Visual Basic Editor
  2. Click Insert → Module in the left panel
  3. Paste the ChatGPT-generated code into the white area
  4. Press F5 or click Run → Run Sub/UserForm to execute
  5. Close the VBA editor — your macro has run on the spreadsheet
Prompt 3 — Automate Report Formatting with VBADemo 3
▶ Prompt
Write an Excel VBA macro that does the following to the active worksheet: 1. Makes the first row (header row) bold, font size 12, with a dark blue background (#003366) and white text 2. Auto-fits all column widths 3. Adds a border around every cell in the range A1 to the last used cell 4. Freezes the top row so it stays visible when scrolling Provide the complete VBA code and add a comment above each step explaining what it does. Make it beginner-friendly.
✦ VBA Code ChatGPT Produces
Sub FormatFinancialReport()
Dim ws As Worksheet
Dim lastRow As Long, lastCol As Long
Set ws = ActiveSheet

' Step 1: Format header row
With ws.Rows(1)
.Font.Bold = True
.Font.Size = 12
.Interior.Color = RGB(0, 51, 102) ' Dark blue
.Font.Color = RGB(255, 255, 255) ' White text
End With

' Step 2: Auto-fit all columns
ws.Columns.AutoFit

' Step 3: Add borders to used range
lastRow = ws.Cells(ws.Rows.Count, 1).End(xlUp).Row
lastCol = ws.Cells(1, ws.Columns.Count).End(xlToLeft).Column
ws.Range(ws.Cells(1,1), ws.Cells(lastRow, lastCol)).Borders.LineStyle = xlContinuous

' Step 4: Freeze top row
ws.Rows(2).Select
ActiveWindow.FreezePanes = True
MsgBox "Report formatted successfully!"
End Sub

Step 4: Show the before/after — a properly formatted financial report in 5 seconds.

Financial metrics (weight: 10%) Provide a score (1–10) and brief rationale for each. Give an overall weighted score and a final verdict: INVEST / PASS / CONDITIONAL (with conditions).
✦ Teaching Points in the Output
The AI will likely flag: (1) Strong traction but first-time CEO is a risk, (2) 8-month runway makes this a "must close now" deal, (3) 180% YoY growth is exceptional, (4) $25M valuation at $800K ARR implies 31x ARR multiple — aggressive but defensible in current AI market. This is the kind of nuanced analysis that real VC associates produce in investment memos.
💡 Classroom Tip
Ask students to change one variable — e.g., reduce the ARR to $200K or change the runway to 3 months — and re-run the analysis. Show how the AI adjusts its verdict. This demonstrates scenario analysis, which is a core VC skill.
