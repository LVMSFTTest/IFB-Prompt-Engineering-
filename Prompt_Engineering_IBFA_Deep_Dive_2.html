The Master Formula

"Acting as a [Role] — do this [Task] — in this [Format]" Add Context ("I am a...")  ·  Size ("max 200 words")  ·  Constraints ("bullet points only")  ·  Iterate ("now add a Risk section")

🎭 Role

Who should the AI be? “equity analyst”, “CFO”, “VC partner”, “pitch coach”, “startup advisor”

📋 Task

What to do? “write a cover letter”, “analyse this startup”, “create a VBA macro”, “screen for stocks”

📐 Format

How to present? “numbered table”, “bullet points”, “max 200 words”, “4 columns: Name|Score|Reason|Risk”

Best Practices to Write Prompts

Before touching any tool, master these 5 techniques. They apply to every tool in this session — same skill, different platform.

The 5 Refinement Levers
#LeverThe Question to AskHow to Fix It
1RelevanceIs there output I don’t need?Add “focus only on X, Y, Z — ignore everything else”
2SizeToo long or too short?Add “maximum 5 bullet points” or “expand to 300 words”
3ContextDoes the AI know who you are?Add “I am a financial analyst preparing a client brief”
4FormatIs the structure right for my use?Add “as a table: Name | Score | Reason”
5IterateCan I build on the last response?“Now add a Risk section to the analysis above”
Demo 1 — Relevance: Show the before & afterLive Demo
Weak Prompt — run this first
Tell me about Google.
What you get — and why it fails
A long paragraph about Google’s founding, Larry Page, HQ in Mountain View, various products … Generic, Wikipedia-level, no financial angle, wrong format. Unusable for professional work.
Strong Prompt — run this next and compare
Acting as a financial analyst covering US tech stocks, give me a structured overview of Alphabet Inc. (Google’s parent company) with exactly these 4 sections, 2 bullet points each:
• Company Background  • Revenue Model  • Market Position  • Top Investment Risk
Keep each bullet to one sentence. This is for an equity research note.
Teaching Tip
Show both outputs side by side. Ask: “Which one could you paste into a client presentation right now?” The difference is 100% in the prompt — same AI, completely different result. This single contrast is the core lesson of the entire session.
Demo 2 — Context: Add your role and watch the output transformLive Demo
Without Context
What are the risks of investing in Tesla?
With Context + Format + Role
I am a buy-side equity analyst at a hedge fund. We are considering a long position in Tesla (TSLA). List the top 5 investment risks I need to present to my investment committee. For each risk include: (1) Risk name, (2) One-line description, (3) Potential stock price impact — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW. Format as a numbered table.
Teaching Tip
The phrase “I am a buy-side analyst at a hedge fund” shifts the entire output from general consumer advice to institutional-grade analysis. “Committee” changes tone. Context is the single biggest upgrade most students can make immediately.
Demo 3 — Chain-of-Thought: Build a professional analyst note in 3 promptsLive Demo
Prompt 1 of 3 — Structure first
I am a financial analyst. I need a brief analyst note on Apple Inc. (AAPL). Step 1: Give me a 5-bullet company overview covering: business model, main revenue segments, market cap context, geographic revenue split, one key competitor.
Prompt 2 of 3 — Layer in financials
Good. Now add a Financial Snapshot section: revenue trend (last 3 years), net profit margin, P/E ratio vs sector average, dividend yield. Same bullet format.
Prompt 3 of 3 — Package it professionally
Combine everything above into a one-page analyst note. Use headers: COMPANY OVERVIEW | FINANCIAL SNAPSHOT | INVESTMENT THESIS (2 buy/hold bullets) | KEY RISK (1 bullet). Add today’s date and “Rating: BUY / HOLD / SELL” at the top. Max 300 words.
Teaching Tip
Time this live — it takes under 3 minutes. A junior analyst at a bank spends 2–4 hours on the same document. This contrast makes the efficiency argument for AI in IB instantly tangible.

Demo 3 — Google / Alphabet: Generic to Analyst-Grade in 4 stepsFull Walkthrough
Step 1 — Broad prompt (show the problem)
Tell me about Google Inc.
Result
A generic paragraph. Wrong format, wrong depth, irrelevant for financial analysis. This is what most beginners submit.
Step 2 — Add Role + Structure (Relevance fix)

ChatGPT + Excel: Formulas & VBA Macros

Generate complex Excel formulas and automate tasks with VBA — all from plain-English descriptions. Two of the highest-value skills in Investment Banking, powered by AI.

Part A: Excel Formula Generator  Free Tier Works

Demo 1 — Financial Ratios: Profit Margin & EPSStep-by-Step
  1. Open ChatGPT_Macros.xlsx alongside ChatGPT. Show the raw dataset: Column A = Company names, B = Revenue, C = Net Profit, D = Shares Outstanding.
  2. Run this prompt in ChatGPT:
    I am working in Excel. My spreadsheet has:
    Column A (A2:A20): Company names | Column B: Revenue (USD) | Column C: Net Profit (USD) | Column D: Shares Outstanding

    Write Excel formulas for:
    1. Profit Margin % = Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100 → put in Column E
    2. Earnings Per Share (EPS) = Net Profit ÷ Shares → put in Column F

    For each: give the formula for row 2 so I can drag it down. Wrap in IFERROR to handle divide-by-zero errors. Explain each formula part in one plain-English sentence.
  3. Copy the formula exactly as written by ChatGPT.
  4. Click cell E2 in Excel. Paste the Profit Margin formula. Press Enter.
  5. Click cell F2. Paste the EPS formula. Press Enter.
  6. Select E2:F2. Drag the fill handle down to row 20. All values populate instantly.
  7. Test the IFERROR: Delete a value in Column B. Show the cell displays “N/A” instead of a #DIV/0! error.
Formulas ChatGPT will generate
E2 (Profit Margin %): =IFERROR((C2/B2)*100, "N/A")
F2 (EPS): =IFERROR(C2/D2, "N/A")
Teaching Tip
Without AI, a new Excel user might spend 30 minutes figuring out IFERROR syntax alone. With AI: describe in English, get working code in 10 seconds. This is the practical ROI of prompt engineering for Excel users.
Demo 2 — Cross-Sheet Lookup: INDEX-MATCH (professional standard)Step-by-Step
  1. Set the scene: You have two sheets — “Companies” (company names + sectors) and “Financials” (company names + revenue). You need to pull each company’s sector into the Financials sheet.
  2. Run this prompt:
    I have an Excel file with two sheets:
    Sheet “Companies”: Column A = company names, Column B = their sector
    Sheet “Financials”: Column A = company names, Column B = revenue

    I need a formula in the Financials sheet, Column C, that looks up each company’s sector from the Companies sheet.
    Use INDEX-MATCH (NOT VLOOKUP — briefly explain in one sentence why INDEX-MATCH is safer). Give the formula for C2 that I can drag down. Include IFERROR for companies not found. Explain each part as if I am a complete Excel beginner.
  3. Paste the formula into Financials!C2 and drag it down.
  4. Show the safety advantage: Insert a blank column between columns A and B in the Companies sheet. VLOOKUP would break. INDEX-MATCH still works perfectly.
Formula ChatGPT will generate
=IFERROR(INDEX(Companies!B:B, MATCH(A2, Companies!A:A, 0)), "Not Found")

MATCH finds the row where your company name appears in Companies column A. INDEX retrieves the sector from column B at that row. This works even if you insert columns later — unlike VLOOKUP.

Part B: VBA Macros  Automate Repetitive Work

How to run any VBA Macro — 5 universal steps
  1. Press Alt + F11 — the Visual Basic Editor window opens.
  2. Click Insert in the menu bar → choose Module from the dropdown.
  3. Paste the ChatGPT-generated VBA code into the white text area on the right.
  4. Press F5 (or click the green ▶ Play button) to run the macro.
  5. Close the VBA Editor (Alt+F4 or X button). The macro has already run on your spreadsheet.
